Project Management

Why this matters: This topic affects real decisions, risk, cost, and long-term options.
📖 The Lesson

Start here

Control scope, schedule, cost, communication, and closeout. The goal is to turn the concept into a repeatable decision process.

What is true?

Write the current facts and documents.

What controls it?

Identify the law, contract, market, or system.

What can change?

Separate assumptions from verified information.

What comes next?

Choose one practical action.

Practical rule: Verify before paying, signing, building, borrowing, or promising.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Acting before verifying.
  • Relying on verbal information only.
  • Ignoring deadlines or ongoing costs.
  • Failing to keep a decision file.
