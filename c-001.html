How Construction Projects Actually Work

Construction is a sequence of decisions, approvals, labor, materials, inspections, and documentation.

The full project path

  1. Define the goal.
  2. Study the property.
  3. Set scope and budget.
  4. Create plans.
  5. Obtain permits.
  6. Schedule labor and materials.
  7. Build in the correct sequence.
  8. Complete inspections.
  9. Finish, document, and close out.
Core idea: Most construction problems begin before anyone picks up a tool.

Three kinds of work

New construction

Building something that did not exist.

Remodeling

Changing an existing structure.

Repair

Restoring something damaged or worn.

Maintenance

Preventing failure and extending useful life.
