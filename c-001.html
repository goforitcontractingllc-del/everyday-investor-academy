C-001 – What Construction Really Is

Lesson

  • Construction is a system, not just labor.
  • Every job has scope, schedule, safety, quality, budget, and communication.
  • Documentation matters.
  • Think before you build.

Field Exercise

Apply this lesson to a real or observed project. Document what you see, identify risks, and describe your next action.
