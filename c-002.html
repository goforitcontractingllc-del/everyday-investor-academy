Reading a Property Before You Build

A builder should understand the land, access, utilities, drainage, structures, and risks before pricing or designing.

What to look at first

Access

Can workers, materials, and emergency vehicles reach the site?

Slope

How will grade affect excavation, foundations, drainage, and cost?

Utilities

Where are water, sewer, septic, power, gas, and communication lines?

Existing conditions

What must remain, be protected, removed, or repaired?

Hidden-cost questions

  • Is there demolition?
  • Are there easements?
  • Will a retaining wall be needed?
  • Is there room for material storage?
  • What happens when it rains?
Builder rule: Never price only what you can see from the driveway.
