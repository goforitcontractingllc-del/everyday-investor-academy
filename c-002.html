📞 Lead Qualification

Project Fit

Does the work fit your license, skills, equipment, service area, schedule, and risk tolerance?

Customer Fit

Who makes the decision? Is the request clear? Is the timing realistic?

Information Before Visit

Address, photos, problem, desired result, occupancy, access, and urgency.

Do Not Quote Yet

A phone description rarely contains enough information for a reliable final price.

🚙 Site Visit

Observe

Existing condition, access, hazards, utilities, adjacent property, weather exposure, and occupants.

Ask

What changed? What result matters? What has been tried? Who approves selections?

Measure

Take enough dimensions to define work and calculate quantity.

Document

Organized photos and notes become the source for scope and estimate.

Open Site Visit Workspace →

📸 Discovery File

Photos

Wide view, medium view, close-up, reference measurements, access, damage, and adjacent property.

Notes

Customer language, contractor observations, unresolved questions, and next action.

Measurements

Label location, unit, method, and verification status.

Unknowns

Separate what is visible from what may require opening, demolition, testing, or professional review.

📋 Scope & Estimate

Scope

Convert discovery into included work, exclusions, assumptions, materials, cleanup, and closeout.

Estimate

Price labor, material, equipment, subcontractors, travel, disposal, overhead, contingency, and profit.

Options

When useful, present repair, replacement, or phased options.

Expiration

Material prices, availability, and schedule capacity can change.

✍️ Agreement & Deposit

Parties

Use correct legal names and project address.

Payment

Deposit, draws, due dates, final payment, and accepted method.

Changes

Written authorization before added work.

Delay

Weather, access, selections, inspection, hidden conditions, and supplier delays.

📦 Planning & Procurement

Dependencies

Permits, inspections, selections, material lead time, utility locates, cure time, and subcontractors.

Procurement

Order the correct product, quantity, delivery date, storage, and handling.

Schedule

Create a realistic sequence rather than one optimistic finish date.

Communication

Tell the customer what must happen before work begins.

Quality Gates

Customer Change

Price + Time

Authorization
