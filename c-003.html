Construction • C-003

Plans, Drawings, and Specifications

Construction documents explain what is being built, where it goes, how it fits together, and what standards apply.

Main drawing types

Site plan

Shows the property, buildings, access, setbacks, and utilities.

Floor plan

Shows rooms, walls, doors, windows, and dimensions.

Elevation

Shows the outside face of the building.

Section

Shows a cut-through view of assemblies and heights.

Specifications

Specifications describe materials, products, quality standards, and installation requirements that drawings may not fully explain.

Practical lesson: A bid should match the plans and specifications—not the contractor's assumptions.
