🧭 Scope Anatomy

Project + Location

Identify exactly where the work occurs.

Existing Condition

Describe visible facts and relevant damage.

Completed Result

State what the finished work will be.

Inclusions + Exclusions

Make the price boundary visible.

Assumptions + Allowances

Identify uncertain conditions or selections.

Change Process

Define how changed work affects price and time.

🏚️ Existing Conditions

Visible Facts

Cracks, rot, stains, movement, missing parts, dimensions, access, and occupant conditions.

Unknowns

Conditions behind finishes, below grade, inside walls, or under flooring.

Evidence

Photos, moisture readings, measurements, and customer history.

Language

Avoid calling an assumption a fact.

✅ Inclusions

Preparation

Protection, moving, masking, demolition, cleaning, and substrate work.

Materials

Product, size, grade, finish, quantity, and who supplies it.

Installation

Method, fastening, layout, and finish.

Closeout

Cleanup, disposal, walkthrough, and records.

🚫 Exclusions

Purpose

Prevent unstated expectations from becoming free work.

Examples

Hidden damage, utility relocation, engineering, survey, painting, permits, or owner-supplied product defects.

Clarity

An exclusion should be specific enough to understand.

Follow-Up

An excluded item can become a change order if later requested.

💵 Allowances

Use

Placeholder budget when final selection or quantity is not known.

Not a Guarantee

Final cost changes when actual selection or quantity differs.

Documentation

State the allowance amount and what it covers.

Customer Choice

Set selection deadlines to protect schedule.

🕳️ Hidden Conditions

Examples

Rot, mold, damaged subfloor, buried concrete, unmarked private utilities, or nonstandard framing.

Process

Stop, document, explain options, price the change, and receive approval.

Responsibility

Clarify whether testing or exploratory work is included.

Risk

Do not continue blindly and hope the problem disappears.

👤 Customer Duties

Access

Keys, gates, parking, work hours, and occupied rooms.

Selections

Products, colors, hardware, and deadlines.

Utilities

Power, water, heat, or temporary service.

Owner-Supplied Materials

Quantity, compatibility, delays, warranty, and shortage risk.

🔄 Change Orders

Description

What is added, removed, or changed?

Reason

Customer request, hidden condition, code, design, or product issue.

Price

Added or credited amount.

Time

Schedule effect and new completion expectation.

🧪 Scope Test

Stranger Test

Can someone who missed the site visit understand the work?

Price Test

Does every included task have labor, material, equipment, and risk accounted for?

Change Test

Can you tell whether a new request is inside or outside the original scope?

Closeout Test

Can you inspect the finished work against the scope?

🛁 Bathroom Floor Example

Request

Replace bathroom floor.

Discovery

Toilet removal, vanity position, trim, transitions, unknown subfloor, owner-supplied flooring.

Scope Decision

Define demolition, disposal, preparation, underlayment, installation, reinstallations, and cleanup.

Hidden Condition

Create a change process for damaged subfloor.
