Surveys, Boundaries, and Easements

Construction depends on knowing where the property begins, where rights cross it, and where work is legally allowed.

Why surveys matter

A survey can show boundaries, corners, improvements, encroachments, easements, and other site facts.

Boundary

The legal edge of the property.

Encroachment

A structure or use crossing a boundary.

Easement

A legal right to use part of the property for a specific purpose.

Setback

Required distance between construction and a property line or feature.

Common easements

  • Utility
  • Driveway
  • Access
  • Drainage
  • Shared road
Do not guess: Fences, trees, and old driveways are not reliable proof of legal boundaries.
