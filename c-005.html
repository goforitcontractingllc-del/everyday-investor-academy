Zoning, Land Use, and Buildability

Zoning tells you what may be allowed, but buildability depends on many additional physical, legal, and financial conditions.

Zoning is only one layer

A property may be zoned for a use while still facing setbacks, access limits, utility problems, slope, flood risk, fire requirements, title restrictions, or cost barriers.

Allowed use

What the zone permits.

Development standards

Height, setbacks, parking, lot coverage, density.

Overlays

Extra rules for hazards, historic areas, airports, floodplains, or other conditions.

Approval path

Ministerial, administrative, conditional, or discretionary review.

Buildability checklist

  • Legal access
  • Water and wastewater
  • Power and utilities
  • Fire access
  • Drainage
  • Soils and slope
  • Budget
Core question: Not just “Can it be built?” Ask “Can it be approved, financed, serviced, and maintained?”
