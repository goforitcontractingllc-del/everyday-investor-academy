⚙️ Tool Setup

Tool Condition

Inspect housing, trigger, guard, cord, battery, handle, and vents.

Accessory

Confirm blade, bit, wheel, or attachment is rated and compatible.

Workpiece

Support and secure material before starting.

Environment

Check lighting, footing, moisture, bystanders, cords, dust, and cut path.

🪚 Circular Saw

Blade Depth

Set only deep enough to clear the material.

Support

Support both sides without trapping the blade or allowing the offcut to tear unpredictably.

Cut Path

Know where the blade, cord, body, and falling offcut will go.

Kickback

Binding, twisting, poor support, or wrong blade can push the saw back toward the operator.

🔩 Drill & Impact

Drill

Better control for drilling and clutch-limited fastening.

Impact Driver

High rotational impact for driving; easy to overdrive or damage fasteners.

Bit Match

Correct size and type reduces cam-out, breakage, and stripped heads.

Control

Start straight, brace for torque, and stop before the fastener damages the material.

🧱 Miter Saw

Stable Base

Saw and material support must be stable and level.

Hands

Keep hands outside the marked danger zone and never cross awkwardly.

Blade

Wait for full speed before the cut and stop before raising through loose offcuts.

Long Material

Support both sides so the work does not lift, tip, or bind.

🌀 Grinder

Wheel Rating

Correct type, diameter, speed rating, and condition.

Guard

Use and position the guard for the task.

Sparks

Direct away from people, combustibles, glass, vehicles, and finished surfaces.

PPE

Eye and face protection, hearing protection, gloves where appropriate, and task-specific respiratory controls.

🌫️ Dust Control

Source Capture

Collect dust where it is created.

Ventilation

Move contaminated air safely without spreading it into occupied spaces.

Housekeeping

Do not dry sweep hazardous dust when a safer method is required.

Respiratory Protection

Selection, fit, training, and program requirements depend on the hazard.

🔌 Electrical Safety

Cords

Inspect for cuts, missing ground pins, crushed sections, and poor repairs.

GFCI

Use required ground-fault protection, especially around temporary power and damp conditions.

Wet Conditions

Do not assume a cordless tool removes every electrical or slipping hazard.

De-Energize

Verify when work may contact electrical systems; licensed scope may apply.

🧾 Power-Tool Maintenance Log

🎮 Power-Tool Challenge

Saw binds in a cut

Release trigger, maintain control, allow blade to stop, and correct support or alignment before restarting.

Cord crosses the cut path

Stop and reroute before cutting.

Guard sticks

Remove the tool from service until corrected.

Dust clouds the room

Pause and improve containment, extraction, ventilation, and PPE.

🌎 Atlas Terms

Kickback

Sudden tool or workpiece movement caused by binding, catching, or loss of control.

GFCI

Ground-fault circuit interrupter designed to reduce shock risk.

RPM Rating

Maximum rotational speed an accessory is designed to withstand.

Guard

Protective device that helps separate the operator from a hazard.

Source Capture

Collecting dust or debris at the point where it is generated.
