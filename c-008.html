👀 Hazard Recognition

Hazard

Something with the potential to cause harm.

Exposure

How a person, property, or environment comes into contact with the hazard.

Control

Action that eliminates or reduces the risk.

Reassessment

Conditions change as the job moves, weather changes, or other trades arrive.

🪜 Falls

Planning

Choose the safest access and work method before carrying tools or material up.

Ladders

Correct type, condition, angle, footing, access, and use.

Openings / Edges

Protect or clearly control exposure.

Housekeeping

Trips near edges or stairs can become serious falls.

⚡ Electrocution

Overhead / Buried

Identify power lines and underground utilities before work.

Temporary Power

Inspect cords, connections, GFCI protection, and wet exposure.

Panels / Wiring

Stay within legal trade scope and verify de-energization.

Metal Tools

Conductive tools and ladders increase danger around electricity.

🚧 Struck-By

Vehicles

Separate people from moving vehicles and equipment.

Falling Objects

Control storage, lifting, overhead work, and exclusion zones.

Flying Particles

Use guards, screens, and eye/face protection.

Material Handling

Secure loads and plan travel paths.

⚙️ Caught-In / Between

Pinch Points

Hands and clothing can be trapped in tools, hinges, loads, and moving parts.

Excavation

Collapse and access hazards require competent planning.

Machinery

Guarding and energy control prevent contact with moving parts.

Loads

Never place the body where a shifting load can trap it.

😷 PPE Selection

Head

Impact and electrical classifications matter.

Eyes / Face

Glasses, goggles, and face shields protect against different hazards.

Hearing

Noise exposure is cumulative.

Respiratory

The respirator must match the hazard and program requirements.

Hands / Feet

Glove and footwear selection should match cuts, chemicals, heat, crushing, and footing.

📝 Site Safety Inspection

🎮 Find the Hazard

Extension cord through standing water

Shock and trip risk. Stop and correct power routing, GFCI, and wet exposure.

Worker cutting overhead without eye protection

Falling debris and particle exposure.

Materials stacked beside a stair opening

Falling-object, trip, and access risk.

Child enters occupied work zone

Stop work and restore separation and supervision.
