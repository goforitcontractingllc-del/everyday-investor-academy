🎯 Define the Deliverable

Completed Result

What will be visibly and functionally complete?

Quality Standard

What drawings, instructions, tolerances, finish, or acceptance criteria apply?

Exclusions

What is outside the project?

Closeout

What records, inspection, cleanup, and handoff are required?

🧩 Break Down the Work

Phase

Large stage such as demolition, rough work, finish, or closeout.

Task

Specific activity with a clear result.

Duration

Reasonable working time—not wishful time.

Owner

Person responsible for completion and communication.

🔗 Dependencies

Finish-to-Start

Task B cannot begin until Task A finishes.

Selection

A product choice may control ordering and installation.

Inspection

Work may not be covered before inspection.

Cure / Dry

Concrete, coatings, adhesives, and compounds require time.

👷 Resources

Labor

Skill, crew size, availability, supervision, and fatigue.

Tools / Equipment

Availability, transport, setup, and maintenance.

Subcontractors

Scope, license, insurance, timing, and handoff.

Access

Keys, occupancy, parking, staging, and work hours.

📦 Procurement

Lead Time

Time between order and usable delivery.

Substitution

Alternative products require compatibility and approval.

Storage

Protect material from theft, weather, damage, and distortion.

Shortage

Plan how missing or damaged material changes sequence.

🏛️ Permits & Inspections

Research Early

Permit requirements can change scope, design, cost, and schedule.

Submittal

Plans, applications, engineering, fees, and corrections take time.

Inspection Hold Points

Schedule work so inspections happen before covering.

Closeout

Final approval may be needed before use or final payment.

🌦️ Weather & Contingency

Forecast

Use it as planning information, not a guarantee.

Material Limits

Temperature, moisture, wind, and rain affect installation.

Site Impact

Mud, access, drying, safety, and protection change productivity.

Contingency

Build realistic float and decision points into the plan.

🗣️ Communication Plan

Start Notice

Confirm access, preparation, timing, and contacts.

Decision Deadlines

Selections and approvals need dates.

Progress Updates

Use a consistent rhythm.

Delay Notice

Explain cause, effect, next action, and revised expectation.

📅 Schedule Builder

🎮 Timeline Challenge

Problem

Paint is scheduled before drywall repair and sanding are complete.

Missing Dependency

Dust-producing work must finish before final cleaning and finish coating.

Material Risk

Flooring delivery is delayed, but baseboard was scheduled immediately after paint.

Fix

Resequence work, confirm lead times, protect completed finishes, and communicate the new critical path.

🌎 Atlas Terms

Critical Path

The sequence of dependent tasks controlling the earliest project completion.

Float

Time a task can move without delaying the project.

Lead Time

Time required to obtain a material, service, or approval.

Milestone

Important point used to measure progress.

Hold Point

Stage where work pauses for inspection, approval, or verification.
