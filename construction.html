What lives here

More than hammers and materials

A successful project depends on what happens before, during, and after physical construction.

🌲 Land and Site

Access, slope, soil, drainage, boundaries, easements, utilities, wildfire, and buildable area.

📐 Design and Planning

Programming, site plans, floor plans, structure, durability, and everyday use.

🏛 Permits and Codes

Zoning, plan review, specialty codes, inspections, corrections, and final occupancy.

💵 Estimating and Contracts

Scope, quantities, labor, materials, overhead, profit, contingency, payments, and changes.

🔨 Building Systems

Foundations, framing, roofing, envelope, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation, and finishes.

🧰 Operations and Longevity

Maintenance, repairs, quality control, warranties, remodeling, and capital planning.

Construction principle: Do not ask only whether it can be built. Ask whether it can be approved, financed, accessed, connected, maintained, and used the way the owner expects.
The complete project path

From question to certificate of occupancy

1 · FeasibilityGoals, property research, zoning, access, utilities, budget, financing, and risk.
2 · DesignSurvey, site plan, architecture, engineering, scope, and specifications.
3 · ApprovalPlanning, permits, plan review, corrections, fees, and utility coordination.
4 · Site WorkClearing, excavation, grading, drainage, utilities, access, and foundations.
5 · StructureFraming, sheathing, roof, windows, exterior enclosure, and weather protection.
6 · SystemsPlumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation, testing, and inspections.
7 · FinishesDrywall, paint, cabinets, flooring, fixtures, exterior work, and landscaping.
8 · CloseoutFinal inspections, punch list, warranties, occupancy, and maintenance plan.

Oregon construction updates belong here

The core lessons stay evergreen. Licensing, code editions, permits, fees, forms, and local interpretations are reviewed separately.

Contractor licensing

Oregon generally requires the appropriate Construction Contractors Board license for construction activity performed for compensation on improvements to real property.

State building codes

Oregon's Building Codes Division adopts and interprets the specialty codes that make up the Oregon State Building Code.

Permits and inspections

Oregon ePermitting supports online applications and inspections for participating jurisdictions; local departments control project-specific services.

Official starting points: Oregon CCB · Building Codes Division · ePermitting
