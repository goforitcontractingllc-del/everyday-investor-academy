Hard and Soft Inquiries

An inquiry is a record that someone accessed your credit report. Hard and soft inquiries happen for different reasons and do not affect credit in the same way.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: People often avoid checking their own reports because they fear hurting their score. Your own review is generally a soft inquiry and does not damage the score.
Just tell me simplyHow it worksWhat to do nowKnowledge check
🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

Not every credit check is a hard inquiry.

A hard inquiry is generally connected to an application for credit, such as a card, auto loan, or mortgage.

A soft inquiry can happen when you check your own report, when a company pre-screens an offer, or during certain account reviews.

Hard inquiries can influence some scores, but their effect depends on the full file, timing, and scoring model.

🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Hard inquiry

Usually linked to a credit application.

Soft inquiry

Review not treated as a new-credit application.

Rate shopping

Certain loan inquiries may be grouped by some scoring models.

Pre-screened offer

A company reviews limited information to make an offer.

Account review

An existing creditor checks the account relationship.

Permissible purpose

A legally allowed reason to access a consumer report.

Lesson
Know the purpose before authorizing access.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Believing your own report check hurts your score.
  • Authorizing applications when you only wanted a quote.
  • Applying repeatedly because the first decision was slow.
  • Assuming all rate-shopping rules are identical.
  • Ignoring an inquiry from a company you do not recognize.
✅ Just Tell Me What to Do

Your action steps

  1. Review the inquiry section on all three reports.
  2. Separate hard inquiries from soft inquiries.
  3. Ask whether a quote requires a hard pull before agreeing.
  4. Group legitimate rate shopping into a short window when practical.
  5. Investigate inquiries you do not recognize.
