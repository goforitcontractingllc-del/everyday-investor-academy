Auto Loans

An auto loan combines the vehicle price, down payment, trade-in, interest rate, term, fees, add-ons, insurance, and the risk that the vehicle can be repossessed after default.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: A low monthly payment can hide a longer term, higher total interest, inflated price, negative equity, or expensive add-ons. Compare the full loan—not just the payment.
Just tell me simplyHow it worksWhat to do nowKnowledge check
🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

Get financing offers before visiting the dealer so you can compare the dealer's offer against a bank or credit-union offer.

Shopping within a focused period can reduce the credit impact because scoring models commonly group same-type loan inquiries.

The vehicle is collateral. Missing payments can create late reporting, collection, repossession, and a remaining deficiency balance.

Clickable terms: Annual Percentage Rate, Negative Equity, Deficiency Balance

🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Amount financed

The dollar amount borrowed after down payment and trade adjustments.

APR

The annualized cost of the credit, including certain finance charges.

Loan term

The number of months scheduled for repayment.

Negative equity

Owing more on a trade-in than it is worth.

Add-ons

Optional products such as service contracts or GAP coverage.

Deficiency balance

The remaining claimed debt after a repossessed vehicle is sold.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Shopping by monthly payment only.
  • Rolling negative equity into the next loan without calculating it.
  • Accepting add-ons without reading cost and cancellation terms.
  • Financing before checking insurance cost.
  • Assuming repossession eliminates the debt.
✅ Just Tell Me What to Do

Your action steps

  1. Pull reports and correct errors before applying.
  2. Get multiple financing offers first.
  3. Keep same-type loan shopping within a focused window.
  4. Compare APR, term, amount financed, and total payments.
  5. Read every add-on and decline products you do not need.
🧪 Knowledge Check
