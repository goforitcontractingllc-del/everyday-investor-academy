Mortgages and Credit

Mortgage approval looks beyond one score. Lenders review credit reports, scores, income, assets, monthly debts, down payment, reserves, property information, and the loan program's rules.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: Preparing early gives you time to correct report errors, lower risky balances, document income, avoid unnecessary applications, and understand the cash needed beyond the down payment.

🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

Mortgage lenders commonly review information from all three major credit bureaus and may use mortgage-specific score models.

Debt-to-income ratio compares monthly debt obligations with gross monthly income, but acceptable limits vary by lender and loan program.

A preapproval is useful, but it is not a final promise to lend. Employment, credit, assets, debts, and the property may be reviewed again before closing.

🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Prequalification

An early estimate based on limited or self-reported information.

Preapproval

A more detailed lender review, still subject to conditions.

Debt-to-income ratio

Monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income.

Cash to close

Down payment plus closing costs and required prepaid items, minus credits.

Reserves

Assets remaining after closing that can cover future payments.

Underwriting condition

A document or requirement that must be satisfied before approval.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Opening new credit before closing.
  • Moving money without keeping a paper trail.
  • Confusing prequalification with final approval.
  • Budgeting only for the down payment.
  • Ignoring taxes, insurance, repairs, and reserves.
✅ Just Tell Me What to Do

Your action steps

  1. Pull all three reports well before applying.
  2. Build income, asset, debt, and tax-document folders.
  3. Calculate a rough DTI and full housing budget.
  4. Compare lenders and loan programs.
  5. Do not change jobs, debts, or bank patterns without discussing it with the lender.
🧪 Knowledge Check
