Why Real Estate Matters

Real estate shapes where we live, work, learn, and build wealth.

It's more than buying houses

Real estate influences nearly every part of life. Homes, apartments, offices, farms, warehouses, hospitals, parks, and shopping centers are all real estate.

Big idea: Even if you never become an investor, understanding real estate helps you make better financial and life decisions.
Housing

Where people live.

Business

Where companies operate.

Community

Schools, roads, parks, and services.

Wealth

One of the most common long-term assets people own.

Remember

Learning real estate is really learning how communities function.
