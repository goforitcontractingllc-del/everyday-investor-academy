What Is Real Estate?

Real estate is land plus the rights and improvements connected to it.

Four parts

Land

The physical ground.

Improvements

Homes, roads, fences, utilities and buildings.

Rights

The legal ability to use, sell, lease, or transfer property.

Location

Often the biggest factor affecting value.

Think of it this way: Real estate isn't just dirt. It's the entire system attached to a piece of land.
