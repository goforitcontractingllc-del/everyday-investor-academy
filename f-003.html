3.1 — Different Starting Points

Two people can want the same result but need different first steps.

One may need credit repair; another may need childcare, licensing, savings, or legal clarity.

3.2 — Capacity Matters

A plan must fit available time, money, energy, transportation, support, and risk tolerance.

A slower plan that survives real life is stronger than a perfect plan that collapses.

3.3 — Build Your Own Sequence

Choose the next step based on urgency, dependency, impact, and available capacity.

Review the sequence as circumstances change.

🚀 Take the Real-World Action

Write your goal, your biggest current constraint, and the smallest next step that fits your real capacity.
