The People Who Make Real Estate Work

Real estate is a team sport involving many different professionals.

Owners

Control the property.

Tenants

Occupy the property.

Property Managers

Operate the property.

Contractors

Build and repair.

Lenders

Provide financing.

Inspectors

Evaluate condition.

Agents

Help buy and sell.

Local Government

Creates and enforces land-use rules.

Lesson: Understanding what each person does makes the entire industry easier to understand.
