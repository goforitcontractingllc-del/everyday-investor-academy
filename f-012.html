Thinking Like an Investor

Investors evaluate opportunities by balancing income, expenses, risk, and long-term value.

Questions investors ask

  • How does this property make money?
  • What could go wrong?
  • What improvements add value?
  • What is the exit strategy?
Great investors are disciplined decision-makers, not gamblers.
