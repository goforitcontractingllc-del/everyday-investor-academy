Reading Public Records

Public records tell the legal story of a property.

Learn to recognize

  • Tax records
  • Assessor information
  • Parcel numbers
  • Ownership history
  • Recorded documents
  • Permit files
Public records often answer questions that listings cannot.
Remember: The strongest learners build systems for finding trustworthy information, not just memorizing facts.
