Mortgages Without the Mystery

Understand principal, interest, term, rate, payment, escrow, down payment, closing costs, and total borrowing cost.

Real-life opening

Why this matters

A mortgage payment is not just a monthly number. It is a long contract combining debt, property, insurance, taxes, fees, and risk.

Quick answer

Understand principal, interest, term, rate, payment, escrow, down payment, closing costs, and total borrowing cost.

Deep dive

Open only what you need.

Principal is the amount borrowed before interest and other costs.

Interest is the cost of borrowing. The rate, balance, and time affect total interest.

The term is the repayment period. A longer term may lower the payment but increase total interest.

Some loans collect taxes and insurance with the monthly payment, then pay those bills from an escrow account.

A down payment reduces the amount borrowed but is not the only cash needed.

Lender, title, appraisal, recording, prepaid, and other costs may be due at closing.

Conventional and government-backed loans have different rules, insurance, and qualification paths.

Do not compare only payment. Compare rate, fees, insurance, cash needed, and long-term cost.
