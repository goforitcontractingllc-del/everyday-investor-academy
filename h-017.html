Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs)

An ADU is a secondary dwelling on the same property as a primary home.

Why ADUs matter

Accessory Dwelling Units increase housing without creating a traditional subdivision. They may provide housing for family members, caregivers, renters, or owners who want additional flexibility.

Evergreen idea: ADUs always depend on local zoning, utilities, setbacks, and permitting. Those changing requirements belong in the Live Atlas.
