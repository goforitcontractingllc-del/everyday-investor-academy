Cottage Clusters

Small homes arranged as one coordinated community.

The big idea

Cottage clusters prioritize people instead of cars by organizing compact homes around shared open space.

Remember: Marketing language and legal land-use classifications are not always the same.

Why planners like them

  • Middle housing
  • Walkability
  • Shared green space
  • Gentle density

Investor lens

Multiple small homes may diversify income but require thoughtful management, utilities, maintenance, and legal review.
