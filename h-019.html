Co-Housing & Shared Living

Intentional communities balance private homes with shared spaces.

How it works

Residents own or lease private homes while sharing amenities such as gardens, workshops, kitchens, or gathering areas.

Benefits

Community support, efficient land use, shared resources.

Challenges

Governance, shared decisions, and maintenance responsibilities.
