Senior & Accessible Housing

Housing should adapt to changing mobility and independence.

Accessibility matters

Accessible housing benefits many people—not only seniors.

  • Step-free entries
  • Wider doorways
  • Safer bathrooms
  • Lighting and navigation
  • Transit and healthcare access
Designing for accessibility often improves usability for everyone.
