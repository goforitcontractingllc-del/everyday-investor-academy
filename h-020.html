Real-life opening

Why this matters

Insurance is not a promise that every loss will be paid. The policy, exclusions, limits, deductible, facts, maintenance, and claim process control the outcome.

Quick answer

Understand coverage, deductibles, exclusions, replacement cost, liability, claims history, and location-based risk.

Deep dive

Open only what you need.

Coverage differs by property type, occupancy, insurer, and endorsements.

A low premium can hide high out-of-pocket exposure.

Market value, loan amount, and rebuild cost are different numbers.

Flood, earthquake, maintenance, vacancy, business use, and other risks may need separate coverage or may be excluded.

Renting, business activity, animals, wood heat, or vacancy can affect coverage.

Photos, receipts, serial numbers, and inventories improve claim records.

Wildfire, flood, wind, smoke, landslide, and access can affect availability and price.

Construction, purchases, household changes, and rising rebuild costs can make old limits inadequate.
