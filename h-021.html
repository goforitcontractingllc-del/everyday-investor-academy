Why this matters

Closing is not just signing papers. It is the point where money, title, loan obligations, insurance, taxes, possession, and records come together.

Quick answer

Deep dive

Open only what you need.

Compare the final disclosure and settlement figures with earlier estimates.

Verify the approved transfer method and guard against wire fraud.

Review ownership, exceptions, liens, easements, and title-insurance information.

Ask questions about loan terms, occupancy, escrow, penalties, and obligations.

Signing, funding, recording, and possession may occur at different times.

Confirm key, code, remote, mailbox, and utility transfer.

Save disclosures, note, deed, title policy, inspection, insurance, tax, and payment records.

Change locks where appropriate, inspect safety devices, update mailing, schedule urgent work, and protect reserves.
