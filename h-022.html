How Housing Is Managed

Housing continues long after construction through maintenance, operations, communication, budgeting, and planning.

Management keeps housing functioning

Whether a property is owner-occupied, rented, or part of an association, someone must coordinate maintenance, budgets, inspections, records, vendors, communication, emergencies, and long-term planning.

Maintenance

Preventive work extends the life of buildings.

Financial planning

Budget for repairs before they become emergencies.

Communication

Owners, residents, contractors, and managers need clear expectations.

Documentation

Keep inspections, invoices, warranties, leases, and permits organized.
