Build a Long-Term Housing Strategy

Create a housing plan that can adapt to income, family, work, health, caregiving, location, and unexpected change.

Beginner friendlyInteractive20–30 minutesPrintable workbook
Real-life opening

Why this matters

A housing decision solves today's problem. A housing strategy also asks what happens after the job changes, the child grows, or a relative needs help.

Quick answer

Create a housing plan that can adapt to income, family, work, health, caregiving, location, and unexpected change.

Deep dive

Open only what you need.

Describe likely household, location, work, school, transportation, and support needs.

Is the goal stability, flexibility, affordability, caregiving, ownership, or downsizing?

Separate emergency stability, credit, reserves, purchase preparation, and repairs.

Moves, rent increases, disability, caregiving, job changes, and children can reshape the plan.

Access, maintenance, transit, storage, and flexible space may matter more over time.

Maintain lease, title, insurance, tax, mortgage, and repair files.

Update costs, goals, risks, household needs, and local options.

Know what would trigger renewal, moving, selling, refinancing, or seeking help.
