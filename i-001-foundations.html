Investment versus purchase

Buying property does not automatically make someone an investor. An investment decision includes a reason for buying, a return path, known costs, risk analysis, and an exit plan.

Consumer purchase

Primarily bought for personal use, comfort, or enjoyment.

Investment purchase

Bought with an intentional plan to create income, equity, appreciation, development value, or another financial return.

Core inputs

  • Cash
  • Credit
  • Time
  • Labor
  • Knowledge
  • Relationships
  • Risk capacity
Remember: An investor is not defined by owning many properties. An investor is defined by how deliberately the decision is made.

Questions before buying

  • Why this property?
  • Why now?
  • How will value be created?
  • What could fail?
  • How long can I hold it?
  • How do I get out?
