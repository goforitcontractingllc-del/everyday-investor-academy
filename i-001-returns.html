How Real Estate Returns Are Created

Separate income, appreciation, equity, loan paydown, improvement value, development value, and tax effects.

Version 2Deep-dive lessonLiving Atlas connected

Seven return channels

Cash flow

Money remaining after operating expenses and debt service.

Appreciation

Increase in market value over time.

Loan paydown

Equity created as principal is repaid.

Forced appreciation

Value created by improving income, condition, use, or efficiency.

Development

Value created through subdivision, construction, entitlement, or change of use.

Tax effects

Potential benefits or obligations that depend on law and the investor's situation.

Important: These channels do not all arrive as spendable cash. Appreciation and equity may exist on paper until a sale or refinance occurs.

Simple example

A rental may produce modest monthly cash flow while the tenant helps pay down the loan and the owner improves the property. The total return can come from several layers at once.
