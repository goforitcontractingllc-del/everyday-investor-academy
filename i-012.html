BRRRR and Refinance Risk

BRRRR attempts to recycle invested cash through rehabilitation, rental stabilization, and refinancing.

18–30 minutes Beginner friendly Detailed lesson Atlas terms included
Why this matters: The strategy is often described as a simple loop, but each stage can fail independently: acquisition, rehab, rent, appraisal, underwriting, or refinance.
📖 The Lesson

The five stages

  1. Buy: Acquire below the property's stabilized value.
  2. Rehab: Complete the work on scope, budget, and schedule.
  3. Rent: Place residents and stabilize income.
  4. Refinance: Qualify for a new long-term loan.
  5. Repeat: Use recovered capital only if the first project remains stable.

The refinance is the hinge

A BRRRR plan depends on value, condition, income, seasoning, appraisal, credit, lender policy, debt-service coverage, and current rates. This is refinance risk.

Cash left in the deal

A successful refinance may recover some, all, or none of the original cash. Investors should calculate cash left in the deal rather than assuming complete recovery.

Stabilization

The property should be safe, functional, legally rentable, properly documented, and operating at a sustainable income level before the strategy is considered stable.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Assuming the appraisal will equal projected ARV.
  • Ignoring refinance fees and prepayment terms.
  • Using short-term debt without a backup hold or sale plan.
  • Repeating before the first property is operationally stable.
📝 Try It Yourself

  • Calculate cash left in a sample deal.
  • Create a backup plan if the refinance returns less cash than expected.
