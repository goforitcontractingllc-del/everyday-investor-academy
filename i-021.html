Building an Investment Team

Real estate decisions improve when the right specialists are involved at the right stage.

18–30 minutesDetailed lessonAtlas terms included
Why this matters: A team is not a pile of business cards. It is a network of qualified people with clear roles, communication, accountability, and verified credentials.
📖 The Lesson

Core team roles

Property and deal

Agent or broker, appraiser, inspector, surveyor, title and escrow professionals.

Money and law

Lender, insurance agent, CPA or tax professional, and attorney.

Physical property

Licensed contractors, engineers, architects, specialty trades, and environmental professionals.

Operations

Property manager, leasing staff, maintenance vendors, bookkeeper, and software support.

Verify before trusting

Check licenses, insurance, references, scope, conflicts, and experience. This is vendor due diligence.

Who represents whom?

Understand agency, compensation, confidentiality, and conflicts. A person involved in a transaction may not automatically owe you every duty you assume.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Choosing only by lowest price.
  • Failing to verify credentials and insurance.
  • Assuming one professional covers every specialty.
  • Not documenting advice, scope, and decisions.
