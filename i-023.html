Creating Your Personal Investment Plan

A personal investment plan connects your goals, finances, skills, timeline, risk capacity, and decision rules.

18–30 minutesDetailed lessonAtlas terms included
Why this matters: Without a written plan, every listing can feel like an opportunity and every setback can change the strategy.
📖 The Lesson

Start with purpose

Define what real estate is supposed to do: reduce housing cost, create income, build long-term equity, support a business, provide family housing, or fund a future goal.

Your buy box

A buy box defines property type, location, price, condition, return, risk, and deal-breakers.

Personal constraints are real inputs

Financial

Income, debt, cash, credit, reserves, and borrowing limits.

Time

Work, parenting, caregiving, travel, and management capacity.

Skill

Construction, analysis, leasing, negotiation, and systems.

Risk

What loss, delay, vacancy, or complexity you can actually survive.

Decision rules

Write minimum reserves, maximum leverage, required return, inspection standards, approval authority, and stop conditions before reviewing deals.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Using someone else's goals.
  • Changing criteria to justify a property.
  • Treating emergency savings as acquisition cash.
  • Choosing a workload your life cannot support.
📝 Try It Yourself
  • Write your purpose statement.
  • Create a buy box.
  • Define minimum untouched reserves.
  • Write three automatic deal-breakers.
