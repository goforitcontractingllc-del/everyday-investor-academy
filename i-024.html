Investing Capstone: From Curiosity to a Real Decision

The capstone combines strategy, analysis, financing, due diligence, operations, risk, and exit into one repeatable decision process.

18–30 minutesDetailed lessonAtlas terms included
Why this matters: Knowing definitions is not enough. The goal is to move from excitement to a disciplined property decision without losing sight of real life.
📖 The Lesson

The complete investment path

  1. Define the goal and buy box.
  2. Choose a strategy that fits.
  3. Research the market and property.
  4. Analyze income, expenses, financing, and reserves.
  5. Inspect physical, legal, financial, and operational facts.
  6. Build expected, bad, and very bad cases.
  7. Confirm management and construction capacity.
  8. Choose the exit and backup exit.
  9. Use written decision rules.
  10. Document what was learned after the outcome.

The investment memo

An investment memo is a short written explanation of the property, strategy, numbers, risks, unknowns, decision, and conditions for proceeding.

Know when not to buy

Passing is an investment decision. A deal that does not fit the plan, numbers, capacity, or evidence is not a missed opportunity.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Buying because of sunk research time.
  • Ignoring one unresolved legal or physical issue.
  • Letting projected upside outweigh survival risk.
  • Failing to record why a deal was accepted or rejected.
