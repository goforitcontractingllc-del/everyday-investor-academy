Do a Land or Development Project

Verify the parcel, site, budget, approvals, team, and exit before committing.

Open district →
1
Confirm parcel identity, ownership, legal description, boundaries, and access.
2
Read zoning, plan designation, overlays, setbacks, density, and approval type.

Verify water, wastewater, power, roads, drainage, fire access, and capacity.

5
Estimate studies, engineering, permits, infrastructure, financing, and holding costs.
6
Create layout, team, timeline, ownership, construction, and exit plans.
