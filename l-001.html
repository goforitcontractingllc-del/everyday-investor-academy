🌎 What Land Really Is

Legal Parcel

A defined property interest shown through deed, legal description, and public records.

Bundle of Rights

Ownership may include use, exclusion, transfer, leasing, and development—subject to law and private restrictions.

Physical Ground

Soils, slope, vegetation, water, hazards, and access shape what is practical.

Connected System

Value depends on location, rights, infrastructure, demand, cost, and timing.

🧭 Land Goals

Live

Primary home, manufactured home, tiny-home path, or rural residence.

Build

House, ADU, cottage cluster, commercial, or mixed project.

Operate

Farm, forest, storage, contractor yard, event use, or another business.

Invest

Hold, entitle, divide, improve, rent, develop, or resell.

📍 Parcel Identity

Tax Lot

Assessor and mapping identifier—not always the legal description.

Legal Description

Recorded text identifying the property.

Owner

Verify current vesting and who has authority to sell.

Address

Useful for navigation, but vacant parcels may lack one or use a nearby reference.

📜 Rights & Restrictions

Deed

Evidence of transfer and vesting.

Easement

Right to use land for access, utilities, drainage, or another purpose.

Lien

Claim connected to a debt or obligation.

Covenant

Private restriction that may be stricter than zoning.

⛰️ Physical Site

Slope

Affects access, excavation, drainage, building, and cost.

Soils

Affect septic, foundation, farming, drainage, and stability.

Water

Flood, wetlands, drainage, and seasonal flow matter.

Vegetation

May signal moisture, habitat, wildfire fuel, clearing cost, or protected resources.

💡 Utilities

Water

Source, legal right, quantity, quality, testing, storage, and cost.

Wastewater

Sewer availability or septic feasibility.

Power

Provider, distance, capacity, easements, and extension cost.

Road / Fire / Internet

Legal access does not guarantee physical or service feasibility.

💰 True Land Cost

Acquisition

Price, closing, title, financing, and taxes.

Predevelopment

Survey, planning, engineering, soils, environmental, and legal.

Infrastructure

Road, driveway, water, wastewater, power, drainage, and fire.

Holding

Interest, taxes, insurance, maintenance, security, and delay.

🧪 Due Diligence

Legal

Title, deed, access, easements, liens, covenants, and ownership.

Planning

Zone, plan designation, overlays, use, standards, and approval.

Physical

Survey, soils, slope, water, hazards, utilities, and access.

Financial

Total cost, timeline, financing, market, contingency, and exit.
