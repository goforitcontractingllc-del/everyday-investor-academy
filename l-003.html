🎯 Write the Goal

Use

What must the land legally support?

Scale

How many units, acres, structures, or operations?

Timing

Immediate use, phased project, or long hold?

Exit

Live, rent, sell, refinance, divide, or develop?

📦 Land Buy Box

Location

Counties, cities, drive time, services, and market.

Parcel

Minimum acreage, shape, frontage, and access.

Regulation

Acceptable zones, overlays, and approval risk.

Money

Price, cash needed, total project budget, and maximum uncertainty.

🔎 Screen Listings

Marketing Claim

‘Buildable,’ ‘utilities nearby,’ and ‘subdividable’ require verification.

Map Match

Confirm the listing points to the correct tax lot.

Public Records

Compare acreage, ownership, zoning, taxes, and structures.

Fast Reject

Remove parcels that fail a nonnegotiable requirement.

🚗 Drive the Area

Approach

Road quality, congestion, bridges, grades, and winter conditions.

Neighborhood

Uses, noise, odors, traffic, upkeep, and future plans.

Services

Fire, schools, grocery, medical, contractors, and utilities.

Market Reality

Does the actual area fit the intended buyer, renter, resident, or business?

📋 Desktop Research

Assessor / GIS

Tax lot, acreage, ownership, taxes, structures, and map layers.

Planning

Zone, plan, overlays, notices, and applications.

Hazards

Flood, wetlands, wildfire, landslide, and environmental data.

Title Clues

Roads, access, easements, subdivisions, and ownership pattern.

👣 Site Visit

Safety

Permission, weather, terrain, wildlife, fire restrictions, and communication.

Walkability

Slope, drainage, vegetation, rock, soil, and access.

Neighbors

Fences, roads, shared systems, conflicts, and surrounding uses.

Document

Photos, GPS points, notes, measurements, and unanswered questions.

🚩 Red Flags

No Legal Access

A physical track is not proof of recorded access.

Utility Assumption

‘At the road’ does not prove capacity or affordable connection.

Wet / Steep / Hazardous

Usable acreage may be much smaller than total acreage.

Pressure

Urgency should not replace title, planning, or site verification.

💬 Questions for Seller

History

How was the land used?

Studies

Survey, septic, well, geotech, environmental, or planning work?

Access / Utilities

Who owns and maintains roads or shared systems?

Problems

Disputes, denials, violations, fire, flood, dumping, or contamination?
