🚙 Legal Access

Recorded Right

Access should be supported by deed, easement, plat, or public road status.

Route

Confirm exact location, width, users, and purpose.

Maintenance

Who pays, controls repairs, gates, snow, and damage?

Finance / Permit

Lenders and agencies may require access standards.

🛣️ Physical Access

Road Surface

Paved, gravel, native soil, seasonal, or private.

Geometry

Width, grade, curves, turnarounds, bridges, and clearance.

Construction

Can concrete trucks, cranes, fire apparatus, and deliveries reach the site?

Cost

Road improvement can dominate the budget.

💧 Water

Public

Verify provider, boundary, capacity, meter, fees, and extension.

Well

Water right questions, drilling risk, depth, yield, quality, power, and storage.

Shared

Agreement, capacity, maintenance, and lender acceptance.

Hauled / Alternative

Legal and operational suitability varies.

🚽 Wastewater

Sewer

Provider, connection point, capacity, elevation, fees, and extension.

Septic

Soils, test, reserve area, setbacks, design, permit, and maintenance.

Density

More units increase wastewater demand.

Failure

No wastewater solution can stop the project.

⚡ Power

Provider

Identify service territory.

Distance

Nearest line does not guarantee connection point or capacity.

Easement

Extension may cross other land.

Cost / Timing

Engineering, transformer, trenching, and lead times matter.

🌧️ Stormwater & Drainage

Upstream

Where does water enter the parcel?

On-Site

Slope, soils, roofs, drives, cuts, fills, and impervious area.

Downstream

Where can water legally and safely leave?

Approval

Local and state rules may apply.

🚒 Fire Access

Road

Width, grade, surface, turnarounds, clearance, and bridges.

Water

Hydrant, tank, pond, or other approved supply may be required.

Address / Response

Location and emergency access affect operations and insurance.

Wildfire

Vegetation, defensible space, construction, and restrictions matter.

🌐 Internet & Services

Provider Map

Coverage maps are not a service guarantee.

Site Test

Verify actual signal and installation.

Business Need

Remote work, cameras, tenant service, and operations may depend on it.

Backup

Cellular, satellite, or redundant service may be needed.
