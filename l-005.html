🗺️ Parcel Facts

Identity

Correct tax lot, owner, legal description, and acreage.

Regulation

Zone, plan designation, overlays, and pending applications.

Physical

Shape, frontage, slope, water, hazards, and usable area.

Money

Taxes, liens, asking price, carrying cost, and transaction terms.

🏗️ Highest & Best Use

Legally Permissible

Allowed or realistically approvable.

Physically Possible

Fits the site and infrastructure.

Financially Feasible

Produces acceptable value or return.

Maximally Productive

Best supported use—not merely the most units imaginable.

💵 Comparable Sales

Location

Market and service area.

Use Rights

Zoning and development potential.

Physical Similarity

Acreage, access, utilities, terrain, and hazards.

Terms / Timing

Cash, financing, distress, improvements, and market date.

🧮 Development Costs

Soft Costs

Planning, survey, engineering, design, environmental, legal, and finance.

Infrastructure

Road, utilities, drainage, fire, grading, and public improvements.

Construction

Buildings, site work, landscaping, contingency, and closeout.

Holding

Interest, taxes, insurance, security, maintenance, and delay.

📈 Income & Exit

Hold

Appreciation, taxes, maintenance, and opportunity cost.

Rent / Operate

Income, vacancy, management, infrastructure, and legal use.

Build / Develop

Construction and absorption risk.

Sell / Divide

Approval, market, transaction cost, and timing.

⚠️ Risk Discount

Unknown Cost

Reduce price or require investigation.

Approval Delay

Time has financing and opportunity cost.

Physical Constraint

Usable area may be much less than gross acreage.

Exit Risk

A narrow buyer pool lowers flexibility.

💰 Maximum Offer

End Value

Conservative value after the intended plan.

All Costs

Development, finance, taxes, selling, and contingency.

Required Return

Compensation for time, capital, and risk.

Residual

What remains is the maximum supported land price.
