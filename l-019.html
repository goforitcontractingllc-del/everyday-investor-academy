Module Overview

This module expands the Academy curriculum into practical developer knowledge using real-world examples, project planning, and decision making.

Why it matters: Understanding this topic reduces costly mistakes during land acquisition and development.

Micro-Lessons

  1. Core concepts explained in plain English.
  2. Developer workflow and professional terminology.
  3. Common mistakes beginners make.
  4. Budgeting and cost considerations.
  5. Questions to ask local agencies and consultants.

Field Scenario

Review a realistic development project. Separate verified facts from assumptions, identify project risks, estimate costs, and decide whether to proceed.

Developer Assignment

  • Research one parcel related to this lesson.
  • Contact or identify the agencies or professionals involved.
  • Create a written go / investigate / no-go recommendation.

Knowledge Check

Review the lesson and complete the quiz before moving to the next module.
