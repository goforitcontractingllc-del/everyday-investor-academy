Overview

This module brings together the concepts learned throughout the Land District and applies them to realistic development decisions.

Focus: Evaluate opportunities, reduce risk, understand costs, and make informed development decisions.

Micro-Lessons

  • Professional terminology
  • Decision-making framework
  • Financial considerations
  • Common pitfalls
  • Developer best practices
  • Action checklist

Real-World Exercise

Analyze a real parcel, identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, risks, projected costs, and recommend whether to proceed, redesign, or walk away.

Developer Assignment

  1. Research one parcel from beginning to end.
  2. Create a written development plan.
  3. Estimate costs and timeline.
  4. Present a final investment recommendation.
