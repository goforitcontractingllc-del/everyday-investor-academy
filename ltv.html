Credit · Atlas Term

LTV

Loan-to-Value Ratio

Plain-English Definition

What it means

A ratio comparing a loan balance with the property's value.

Formula or structure

LTV = Loan Amount ÷ Property Value × 100

Example

A $240,000 loan on a $300,000 property has an 80% LTV.

Why it matters

Lenders use LTV to measure collateral risk and determine pricing or mortgage insurance requirements.

Common mistakes

  • Using purchase price when the lender uses appraised value.
  • Ignoring combined liens.
  • Assuming value is guaranteed.
