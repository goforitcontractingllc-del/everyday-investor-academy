Net Worth

Net worth is what you own minus what you owe. It measures financial position at one moment, not character, income, or monthly stability.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: Income shows movement. Net worth shows accumulation. A person can earn a lot and own very little—or earn modestly while steadily building assets.

🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

Assets have financial value. Liabilities are debts or obligations.

Net worth can be negative while someone is still making meaningful progress.

🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Liquid asset

Cash or an asset that can be accessed quickly.

Long-term asset

Property or accounts intended to be held over time.

Secured debt

Debt backed by collateral.

Unsecured debt

Debt not tied to specific collateral.

Equity

Asset value remaining after related debt.

Net-worth statement

A dated list of assets and liabilities.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Using original purchase price instead of current value.
  • Counting future income as an asset.
  • Leaving out delinquent or family debt.
  • Treating negative net worth as failure.
  • Comparing your net worth with someone else's incomplete story.
