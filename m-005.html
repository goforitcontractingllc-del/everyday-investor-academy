Financial Priorities

Financial priorities decide what gets protected first when money is not enough for everything at once.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: Paying the loudest caller first can leave housing, utilities, transportation, insurance, taxes, or legal deadlines exposed.
Just tell me simplyHow it worksWhat to do nowKnowledge check
🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

Priority is based on consequence, deadline, and safety—not shame or pressure.

Protect immediate safety and the systems that keep income and housing functioning.

Some debts are important but less urgent than eviction, shutoff, uninsured driving, court deadlines, or lost employment.

Clickable terms: Financial Triage, Priority Debt, Critical Deadline
⚙️ How the System Works
Protect safetyFood, medicine, shelter, immediate danger.
Protect stabilityUtilities, transport, insurance, childcare, work tools.
Protect deadlinesCourt, tax, license, and written notices.
Then optimizeDebt strategy, savings, and longer-term goals.
🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Essential need

A cost tied to safety, housing, food, health, or basic function.

Critical deadline

A date connected to legal, service, coverage, or employment consequences.

Secured debt

Debt that can lead to loss of collateral.

Priority debt

A debt with unusually serious consequences if ignored.

Negotiable expense

A cost that may be reduced, delayed, or changed.

Triage

Sorting problems by urgency and consequence.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Paying unsecured debt before preventing eviction or shutoff.
  • Ignoring legal documents.
  • Treating every bill as equally urgent.
  • Using all cash for debt with nothing for food or fuel.
  • Making promises without checking the actual cash available.
✅ Just Tell Me What to Do

Your action steps

  1. List every bill and deadline.
  2. Write the consequence of nonpayment.
  3. Mark safety, housing, work, legal, and insurance risks.
  4. Contact providers before deadlines when possible.
  5. Assign available money in priority order.
🧪 Knowledge Check

1. Should the loudest collector always be paid first?

2. Do legal deadlines require attention?

3. Should food and housing be included in triage?
