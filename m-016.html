Credit Scores

A credit score is a number created from information in a credit report. It estimates lending risk, but it does not measure income, character, or whether a loan is affordable.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: A score can affect approval, interest rate, deposit requirements, and borrowing cost, but different lenders may use different models, versions, and credit reports.
Just tell me simplyHow it worksWhat to do nowKnowledge check
🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

You do not have one universal credit score.

Payment history, revolving balances, account age, account mix, and recent applications can influence many scoring models.

The report behind the score matters more than chasing one number from one app.

Clickable terms: Credit Score, Credit Utilization, Underwriting
⚙️ How the System Works
Report data updatesBalances, payments, accounts, or inquiries change.
A model is selectedThe lender or app chooses a score model.
The score is calculatedThe formula reads the report at that moment.
The lender decidesIncome, debts, collateral, and policy may also matter.
🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Credit report

The record containing reported accounts and payment history.

Scoring model

The formula used to calculate a score.

Score version

A particular edition of a scoring model.

Utilization

Reported revolving balances compared with limits.

Hard inquiry

A credit check generally tied to an application.

Underwriting

The lender’s full decision process beyond the score.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Treating one app score as the only real score.
  • Applying repeatedly after a denial.
  • Paying interest just to build credit.
  • Ignoring the reports behind the scores.
  • Confusing approval with affordability.
✅ Just Tell Me What to Do

Your action steps

  1. Pull all three reports.
  2. Label every score by source, model, bureau, and date when available.
  3. Pay active accounts on time.
  4. Reduce risky revolving balances as cash allows.
  5. Check lender requirements before a major application.
