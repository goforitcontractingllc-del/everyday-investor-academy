Loans and Interest

A loan is borrowed money repaid under agreed terms. Interest, fees, term, payment structure, and collateral determine how expensive and risky the loan is.

25–35 minutesBeginner friendlyPlain EnglishDetailed lessonAtlas connected
Why this matters: A low monthly payment can hide a longer term, more interest, higher fees, or a balloon payment.
Just tell me simplyHow it worksWhat to do nowKnowledge check
🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

Principal is the amount borrowed. Interest is the price of using the lender’s money.

APR is a standardized annualized measure that includes interest and certain finance charges.

The total of payments shows how much the borrower is scheduled to repay over the full term.

Clickable terms: Loan Principal, Annual Percentage Rate, Amortization
⚙️ How the System Works
BorrowThe lender provides money or pays a seller.
RepayScheduled payments begin.
Interest accruesThe cost depends on balance, rate, and terms.
Loan closesThe balance is paid, refinanced, settled, or otherwise resolved.
🧩 The Parts You Need to Recognize

Principal

The amount borrowed.

Interest rate

The percentage charged for borrowing.

APR

The annualized cost of credit including certain finance charges.

Loan term

The time allowed for repayment.

Amortization

The schedule dividing payments between principal and interest.

Collateral

Property securing the loan.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Shopping only by monthly payment.
  • Ignoring origination or add-on fees.
  • Assuming longer terms are cheaper.
  • Using secured debt without understanding collateral risk.
  • Signing before reviewing prepayment or balloon terms.
✅ Just Tell Me What to Do

Your action steps

  1. Write the amount borrowed and exact purpose.
  2. Compare APR, term, fees, and total payments.
  3. Check whether the loan is secured.
  4. Read late, prepayment, and balloon provisions.
  5. Test the payment against a conservative budget.
