Creating Your Personal Money Plan

🧠 Just Tell Me Simply

A written money plan combines spending, saving, debt, investing, and goals into one system you can review regularly.

⚙️ Why It Matters

Long-term financial success is built through consistent systems, informed decisions, and regular review.

✅ Action Steps

  1. Review this lesson.
  2. Write one change you will make this month.
  3. Update your written money plan.
  4. Review progress monthly.

🌍 Living Atlas Terms

Cash Flow, Net Worth, Risk, Financial Independence

🧪 Knowledge Check

  2. Should you review your plan regularly?
  3. Can financial goals change over time?
