📖 The Lesson

Start with the real numbers

Build one complete personal money plan from all 23 lessons. A useful money system begins with what is true today—not what should be true in a perfect month.

Income

What comes in, how often, and how reliable it is.

Obligations

Housing, utilities, food, transportation, taxes, debt, and other required costs.

Protection

Reserves, insurance, records, and backup plans.

Goals

What you are building toward and what it requires monthly.

Practical rule: Do not judge the number before you understand the number.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Using guesses instead of records.
  • Ignoring irregular expenses.
  • Building a plan with no review date.
  • Using credit as the only emergency plan.
