Oregon Licensing, Regulation, and the Exam Path

Oregon property management is regulated professional real estate activity. The application, approved education, examination, background check, business association, and trust-account duties all matter.

🧠 Explain It Like I’m New

Oregon currently requires applicants to apply through eLicense, complete an approved 60-hour pre-license course, pass the property-manager exam, and complete fingerprinting and a background check.

A person may hold only one Oregon real estate license category at a time.

This Academy is supplemental and does not create exam eligibility.

Clickable terms: Pre-License Education, Registered Business Name, Background Check

🧩 What You Must Recognize

Oregon Real Estate Agency

The state licensing and regulatory agency.

eLicense

The state online licensing system.

Approved course

The official 60-hour pre-license education.

PSI exam

The licensing examination through the state testing provider.

Background check

Fingerprint-based review for applicants.

Registered business name

The approved business under which licensed activity is conducted.
