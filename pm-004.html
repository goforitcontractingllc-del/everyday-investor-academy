Agency, Fiduciary Duty, Ethics, and Judgment

A property manager is the owner’s agent, but must also handle money accurately, treat residents lawfully, protect confidential information, disclose conflicts, and refuse illegal instructions.

45–70 minutesScenario basedExam preparationOregon focused

Why this matters: The hardest questions usually ask what the manager should do when duties conflict or pressure is applied.
🧠 Explain It Like I’m New

Agency means acting for the owner within granted authority.

Fiduciary responsibility includes loyalty, care, accounting, disclosure, confidentiality, and obedience to lawful instructions.

Blind obedience is not professional loyalty.

Clickable terms: Agency, Fiduciary Duty, Conflict of Interest

🧩 What You Must Recognize

Principal

The owner granting authority.

Agent

The manager acting for the owner.

Loyalty

Acting for lawful owner interests.

Accounting

Safeguarding and reporting money and property.

Disclosure

Providing material information needed for decisions.

Confidentiality

Protecting information from improper sharing.

🏢 Field Scenario

The owner says, “Do not rent to anyone with children.”

Open the professional walkthrough
  1. Recognize a serious familial-status fair-housing concern.
  2. Obedience applies only to lawful instructions.
  3. Refuse to implement the instruction.
  4. Document the request and response.
  5. Escalate under company policy and obtain qualified guidance.
🧪 Exam-Style Check

1. Does obedience include unlawful instructions?

2. Is accounting a fiduciary duty?

3. Should conflicts be disclosed?

4. Should judgment be documented?
