PM-009 · Property Management

Move-Ins

Create a clear starting record with condition documentation, keys, utilities, orientation, and resident communication.

20–30 minute readEvergreen coreReviewed July 2026

Start here

Move-in establishes the starting condition, resident access, utility setup, safety baseline, and evidence that will matter throughout the tenancy.

Before keys are released

The property should be clean, safe, functional, and ready for use. Promised work should be complete or documented. The lease should be signed, required funds handled, and utilities addressed.

Rent-ready standard

  • Doors, windows, and locks function.
  • Smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms are addressed.
  • Plumbing has no active leaks.
  • Electrical fixtures and provided appliances are tested.
  • Heating and essential systems operate.
  • Trash and prior belongings are removed.
  • Access and assigned areas are usable.

Condition documentation

Use written notes, wide photos for room context, and close-ups for cracks, stains, chips, wear, missing items, or damage. Label files by property, unit, room, and date.

Think ahead: Could someone who was not present understand the condition two years later?

Resident additions

Explain how and when the resident can add condition items. Acknowledge additions, save evidence, and create work orders where needed. Documenting a defect does not remove the duty to address it.

Keys and access

  • Unit and building keys.
  • Mailbox and storage keys.
  • Garage remotes and gate devices.
  • Parking permits.
  • Common-area access.

Record quantity, recipient, date, and replacement responsibility.

Orientation

Money

Portal, due date, receipts, and payment questions.

Maintenance

Request channel, emergency definition, shutoffs, and after-hours contact.

Property

Parking, trash, laundry, yard, filters, breakers, alarms, and shared spaces.

Communication

Office hours, response expectations, notices, inspections, and contact updates.

Utilities and rural systems

Confirm who holds each account and the effective date. Rural homes may need instructions for wells, septic, propane, pumps, generators, water treatment, and private trash.

Common mistakes

  • Keys before complete paperwork or funds.
  • Only general photos.
  • No key receipt.
  • Undocumented promised work.
  • Skipped safety checks.
  • No maintenance or emergency instructions.

Practice exercise

Walk through your own home as if documenting move-in. Build a room-by-room photo list, access-device list, and five instructions needed in the first 48 hours.

Check your understanding

  1. Why use wide and close-up photos?
  2. Why record keys?
  3. How is documentation different from repair?
  4. What should the resident know immediately?
Open the answer guide

Wide photos show context and close-ups show detail; key records prove possession and quantity; documentation preserves evidence but does not correct the condition; explain access, money, utilities, maintenance, emergencies, safety, and communication.

Oregon update note

Current through July 2026: Move-in practices interact with Oregon rules on agreements, deposits, essential services, habitability, access, and documentation. A checklist is evidence but does not waive statutory duties or resident rights.
