Property Management • PM-012

Repairs & Work Orders

Every repair should follow a consistent process from report to completion.

  1. Receive the request.
  2. Determine urgency.
  3. Create a work order.
  4. Assign a qualified vendor.
  5. Track progress.
  6. Confirm completion.
  7. Document cost and outcome.
Emergency examples: Gas leaks, flooding, fire, no heat during dangerous weather, major electrical hazards.

Documentation

Every work order should include the date, unit, problem, photos when appropriate, vendor, invoice, and completion notes.
