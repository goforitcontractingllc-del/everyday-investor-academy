Property Management • PM-019

Capital Improvements

Major improvements extend the useful life or value of a property.

Examples

Roofs, HVAC, windows, paving, major remodels.

Planning

Estimate costs, timing, permits, and disruption.

Capital improvements differ from routine maintenance because they usually provide long-term value.
