District 7 · Property Management

Learn how rental properties are actually operated.

Property management combines housing, maintenance, money, communication, documentation, law, construction, and daily problem solving. This district teaches how those pieces work together.

Begin with PM-001View all 24 lessons
What lives here

The operating side of real estate

A rental property can look simple from the outside. Behind the scenes, someone must handle leasing, repairs, records, inspections, money, communication, vendors, compliance, and long-term planning.

🏢 Daily Operations

Learn how managers organize communication, tasks, records, inspections, payments, and recurring responsibilities.

📋 Leasing

Understand marketing, applications, screening, leases, deposits, renewals, move-ins, notices, and move-outs.

🔧 Maintenance

Learn preventive maintenance, emergency response, work orders, vendors, turnovers, inspections, and capital planning.

💵 Property Money

Track rent, deposits, invoices, operating expenses, reserves, budgets, owner funds, and property performance.

⚖️ Compliance

Build consistent practices around Fair Housing, documentation, notices, habitability, access, and state-specific requirements.

🛠 Construction Connection

Understand when routine maintenance becomes a repair project, remodel, capital expense, permit issue, or contractor job.

Academy structure: These lessons teach the evergreen operating principles. Current Oregon laws, official forms, notices, licensing rules, and local resources belong in the Living Atlas so they can be updated without rewriting the core curriculum.

Leasing and Tenancy

PM-008 and PM-011 through PM-015 cover marketing, screening, leases, rent, deposits, and resident transitions.

Money and Planning

MODULE 5

Rights and Documentation
