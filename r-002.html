Know Your Rights District · R-002

Federal, State, and Local Laws

Understand which level of government creates the rule and why the exact location and subject matter matter.

18–30 minutes Beginner friendly Detailed lesson Atlas terms included
Why this matters: A rule that applies in one city may not apply in the next, while federal protections may apply across the country.
Important: This lesson teaches general legal literacy. Laws, deadlines, exceptions, and local rules change. Verify the exact rule with an official source or qualified legal professional before acting.
📖 The Lesson

Federal law

Federal law can create nationwide protections in areas such as fair housing, consumer credit, disability rights, and discrimination.

State law

Oregon law adds rules for housing, contracts, property, employment, licensing, and consumer protection. State law may provide protections beyond federal law.

Local law

Cities and counties may regulate zoning, permits, short-term rentals, business licenses, code enforcement, and some housing issues. Local authority is called jurisdiction.

Which rule controls?

Sometimes several rules apply at once. The exact answer depends on the subject, location, date, type of property, parties involved, and whether a more protective local rule is permitted.

Start with the correct address

Before researching, confirm whether the property is inside city limits, in unincorporated county territory, or subject to another special district.

⚠️ Common Mistakes
  • Researching the wrong city or county.
  • Assuming state law is the only applicable rule.
  • Using an old summary after the law changed.
  • Ignoring special rules for subsidized, manufactured, or federally connected housing.
