Tenant Rights and Responsibilities in Oregon

Learn the basic structure of Oregon rental agreements, habitability, access, notices, documentation, and tenant responsibilities.

18–30 minutes Beginner friendly Detailed lesson Atlas terms included
Why this matters: Oregon landlord-tenant law is detailed and changes over time. A general summary is useful only when paired with the current statute and the exact facts.
Important: This lesson teaches general legal literacy. Laws, deadlines, exceptions, and local rules change. Verify the exact rule with an official source or qualified legal professional before acting.
📖 The Lesson

The rental agreement

Oregon law governs residential rental agreements for dwelling units located in the state. Written agreements, amendments, notices, and payment records should be preserved.

Habitability and repairs

Landlords generally have duties concerning the condition and habitability of rental housing. Tenants also have duties related to use, cleanliness, safety, and avoiding damage. The exact remedy for a repair problem depends on the issue and required notice.

Access

Oregon law regulates when and why a landlord or agent may enter areas under a tenant's exclusive control. Emergency, requested repair, service, inspection, and showing situations may have different rules.

Notices and service

A notice is not only a message. The content, timing, method of service, and legal basis may all matter. A written notice should be kept with proof of delivery.

Tenant responsibilities

Common responsibilities include paying rent, following lawful agreement terms, using the property reasonably, reporting problems, and avoiding damage or dangerous conduct.

Do not self-diagnose a notice: Get current legal help quickly when facing termination, eviction, serious habitability issues, discrimination, or safety concerns.
