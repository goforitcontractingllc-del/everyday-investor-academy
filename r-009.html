Contractor Rights and Responsibilities

Licensing, written contracts, payment, change orders, liens, complaints, warranties, and documentation.

18–30 minutesDetailed lessonEducational—not legal advice
Why this matters: Construction disputes usually start with unclear scope, undocumented changes, missing notices, or expectations never put in writing.
Important: Verify current law, deadlines, local rules, and professional advice before acting.
📖 The Lesson

Start Here

Verify CCB and specialty licensing before work begins.

Use a detailed written scope, payment schedule, materials list, and responsibility chart.

Document every change in price, scope, material, and completion time before performing it.

Practical rule: Write down the facts, preserve proof, find the official rule, and identify the remedy before taking an irreversible step.
