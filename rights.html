The complete path

From problem to informed action

1 · Name itHousing, contract, discrimination, property, debt, benefits, or safety.
2 · Find authorityLaw, rule, lease, contract, title document, policy, or order.
3 · Preserve factsTimeline, photos, messages, notices, receipts, and proof of delivery.
4 · Check deadlinesNotice periods, response dates, hearings, appeals, and filing limits.
5 · Choose processWritten request, complaint, inspection, mediation, appeal, or court.
6 · Protect safetyEmergency services, habitability, fraud controls, or immediate help.
7 · EscalateAgency, advocate, inspector, licensed professional, mediator, or attorney.
8 · Save outcomeAgreement, order, repair, payment, decision, and future duties.

Current laws, forms, agencies, and deadlines

The core lessons teach the system. Legislation, court decisions, agency rules, local ordinances, and forms must be checked before acting.

Oregon landlord-tenant law

ORS Chapter 90 is the official statutory starting point. Oregon’s Legislative Policy and Research Office published an April 2026 rights-and-responsibilities guide.

Oregon fair housing

Oregon BOLI describes fair housing as the right to choose and live in housing free from unlawful discrimination.

Federal fair housing

HUD explains that the Fair Housing Act covers renting, buying, mortgages, housing assistance, advertising, and other housing-related activity.

Disability laws

The correct law depends on the setting. The Fair Housing Act covers many housing situations; the ADA applies to some housing programs and public-facing areas.

Official starting points: ORS Chapter 90 · Oregon 2026 Guide · Oregon BOLI · HUD Fair Housing · ADA Guide
